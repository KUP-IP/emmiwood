End users opt in on the Emmiwood Barbers booking website at https://emmiwood-barbers-preview.pages.dev/emmiwood/book (production will use the shop website). When booking, they enter their mobile number and may optionally check a box agreeing to receive appointment confirmation, reminder, and update texts from Emmiwood Barbers. Consent is not required to complete a booking. Opt-in language and SMS terms are shown at booking and at https://emmiwood-barbers-preview.pages.dev/emmiwood/sms-terms, and the privacy policy is at https://emmiwood-barbers-preview.pages.dev/emmiwood/privacy. That policy explains data collection and states that mobile phone numbers and messaging consent are not shared, sold, or provided to third parties or affiliates for marketing or promotional purposes. Message frequency varies. Message and data rates may apply. Customers reply STOP to opt out or HELP for help. Staff admin one-time passcodes are sent only to allowlisted administrator mobile numbers for portal security, not marketing.
