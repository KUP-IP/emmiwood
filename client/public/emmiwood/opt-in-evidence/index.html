Optional appointment text opt-in (website)

This page documents the customer SMS consent control shown during online booking at https://www.emmiwood.com/emmiwood/book. The messaging brand is KUP Solutions. Consent is optional and is not required to complete a booking. The checkbox is unchecked by default.

Consent control (as shown on the booking form)

What messages customers may receive

Transactional only, from KUP Solutions: booking confirmations, appointment reminders, reschedule and cancellation notices for the appointment they booked or manage. A hosted business name (for example Emmiwood Barbers) may appear as appointment details. Message frequency varies based on appointments and updates. Message and data rates may apply. Customers reply STOP to opt out or HELP for help.

Privacy and terms

  • SMS privacy: https://kup.solutions/sms/privacy
  • SMS terms: https://kup.solutions/sms/terms

The KUP Solutions SMS privacy policy states that mobile phone numbers and messaging consent are not shared, sold, or provided to third parties or affiliates for marketing or promotional purposes.

Staff portal codes (not marketing)

Separately, one-time staff sign-in codes may be sent by KUP Solutions only to allowlisted administrator mobile numbers for shop portal security. That is not a customer marketing program.
