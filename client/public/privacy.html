Privacy notice

Emmiwood Barbers · 1118 S Minnesota Ave, Sioux Falls, SD 57105 · (605) 900-6334

Emmiwood Barbers (“Emmiwood,” “we,” “us”) operates the shop booking website at 1118 S Minnesota Ave, Sioux Falls, SD 57105. This notice explains booking information collected on this website.

Information we collect

When you book or manage an appointment we may collect: your name, mobile phone number, appointment details (service, barber, date and time), optional notes you provide, SMS opt-in status if you choose appointment texts, and technical records needed to run the booking system (for example request logs and audit events).

How we use information

We use this information to schedule and manage appointments, prevent double-booking, support cancellation or rescheduling, operate shop administration, and provide technical support for the booking system.

Appointment texts

Optional appointment confirmation and reminder texts are sent by KUP Solutions, not by Emmiwood Barbers as a separate messaging brand. If you opt in, KUP Solutions is the sender.

  • SMS privacy: https://kup.solutions/sms/privacy
  • SMS terms: https://kup.solutions/sms/terms

We do not share, sell, or provide your mobile phone number or messaging consent data to third parties or affiliates for marketing or promotional purposes. Consent is optional and is not required to complete a booking.

Access

Authorized Emmiwood staff may access booking data to run the shop. KUP Solutions hosts the booking technology and sends opted-in appointment texts. Infrastructure vendors process data only as needed to provide those services.

Retention

Booking records are retained for shop operations, customer support, security, and legal requirements, then removed or minimized when no longer needed.

Contact

Questions about a booking: call the shop at (605) 900-6334. Questions about appointment texts: isaiah@kup.solutions.
